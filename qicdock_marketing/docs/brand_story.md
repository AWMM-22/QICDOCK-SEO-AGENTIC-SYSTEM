# QicDock Brand Story

## Made for the Way Life Moves.

We believe technology should make life easier, not add to what you think about. QicDock was born from a simple idea: create products that fit naturally into your life, your car, and your everyday moments.

## Designed to Adapt

QicDock combines sleek design with a modular approach, giving you the freedom to customise your setup as your needs change. Clean, smart, and built to evolve with you.

From your car to your desk, office to home, we're building products that move with you. Wherever you are, whatever your day looks like, QicDock is made to fit into it effortlessly.

## Built to Fit Your Drive

Every car is different. QicDock offers universal, car-specific and custom docks, designed to give every car the right fit.

## Built for Better Charging

One QicDock charger, multiple ways to use it. Choose the stand that fits your space — from your car console and AC vent to your table or wall. Our modular stands are designed to give your QicDock the right setup, wherever you need to charge.

## Charge Smarter On the Go

QicDock, the only car-specific, custom-fit Magnetic dock designed to keep your iPhone charging completely hands-free while you're on the road. Engineered to fit seamlessly into the centre console of your car, this innovative charging pad securely snaps your Magnetic-compatible iPhone into place, eliminating the hassle of tangled cables or bulky mounts.

## The Cable Mess We All Know.

Your car may be designed for convenience, but charging your phone often means tangled cables, loose wires, and cluttered consoles. Every drive becomes another round of plugging in, unplugging, and finding somewhere to keep the cable. QicDock removes that friction. It keeps your phone charging without the cable hassle, giving it a dedicated place while keeping the console cleaner, simpler, and more organised.

Just dock, charge, and drive.
